LayerLens is a free, open-source viewer for Windows. Scrub through layers, check print time and filament, and catch problems before the first layer goes down.

What it does

A G-code file is a long list of instructions. LayerLens turns it into something you can look at, so you catch problems on screen instead of halfway through a 14-hour print.

See every layer

Drag the slider to any height. The toolpath updates as you move, so you can check supports, seams and bridges before printing.

Colour by feature

Outer walls, inner walls, infill, support and travel moves each get their own colour, taken from the slicer's own labels.

Know the cost

Print time, filament length and weight, and travel distance, with the source of each number shown so you know what to trust.

Play it back

Animate the print at any speed to follow the nozzle through the sequence, and spot wasted moves or odd paths.

Private by design

Your files stay on your machine. No account, no uploads, no telemetry. The goal is to work with no internet connection at all.

Open source

MIT licensed, written in Rust. Read the code, report a bug, or add support for your slicer or printer.

How it works

  1. 1

    Open the file

    Drag a .gcode file onto the window, or use File, Open. Large files are supported as a goal for v1.0.

  2. 2

    Scrub the layers

    Move the slider or press play. Look for supports in the wrong place, long travels and anything that doesn't match your model.

  3. 3

    Check the numbers

    Compare the time and filament against what you expected, then send the job to your printer with confidence.

Roadmap

LayerLens is being built in the open. Here is where it stands.

  1. Done

    Plan

    Product requirements, technical design and test samples are written and public.

  2. In progress

    Foundations

    Project setup, the G-code parser and a test suite that checks it against known results.

  3. Next

    2D viewer

    Open a file, step through layers from the top down, and see the first summary numbers.

  4. Planned

    3D view and stats

    Orbit the model, colour by feature, and show time and filament with playback.

  5. Planned

    v1.0 release

    A single Windows download, tested on sample files from several slicers.

  6. Later

    Compare versions

    See what changed between two slices, layer by layer.
